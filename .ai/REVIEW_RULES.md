# Review Rules

Review from authority to evidence:

1. identity, status, and owner
2. governing Foundation, ADR, and specification
3. ontology and schema meaning
4. dependency direction and interface boundaries
5. traceability and impact
6. Workspace, security, privacy, and AI authority
7. compatibility, migration, failure, and recovery
8. tests and retained evidence
9. approval authority

Unknown is not pass. A green tool result does not override semantic, security, isolation, or authority failure.

For Agents and Skills, also review triggers, Tool authority, budgets, no-progress termination, sandbox cleanup, repeated-trial variance, oracle hierarchy, Judge independence, holdout contamination, failure classification, adversarial cases, and GOV-012 evidence.
