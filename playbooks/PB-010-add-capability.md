# PB-010: Add a Capability

1. Complete Discovery before asking for unavailable information.
2. Define problem, users, owner, outcomes, scope, non-goals, and risks.
3. Update product requirements and business analysis.
4. Extend ontology only when existing concepts cannot express the meaning.
5. Create Product spec, then Architecture, Interfaces, Components, Runtime, and tests in dependency order.
6. Keep rules deterministic and plugins provider-only.
7. Update graph, matrices, indexes, examples, playbooks, and AI guidance.
8. Accept only after end-to-end traceability and quality gates pass.
