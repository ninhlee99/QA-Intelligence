# PB-007: Breaking Change

1. Declare breaking status before implementation.
2. Identify authority, affected artifacts, owners, consumers, Workspaces, data, history, and releases.
3. Define new version, compatibility window, migration, rollout, rollback, and communication.
4. Preserve historical interpretation and evidence.
5. Validate old, transition, and target states.
6. Obtain Architecture, Product, Security/AI/Knowledge specialists as applicable.
7. Release only after GOV-007 and GOV-009 gates pass.
