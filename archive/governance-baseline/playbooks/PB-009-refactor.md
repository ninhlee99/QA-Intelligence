# PB-009: Refactor

1. State the unchanged behavior and improvement objective.
2. Identify contracts, consumers, invariants, and historical evidence.
3. Add characterization and architecture tests before structural change.
4. Preserve interface, schema, rule, Workspace, security, and evidence semantics.
5. Perform impact analysis even when behavior is intended unchanged.
6. Validate equivalence and operational characteristics.
7. If behavior or contract changes, reclassify through PB-007.
