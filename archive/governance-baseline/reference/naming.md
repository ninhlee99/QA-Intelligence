# Naming

- specifications: `SPEC-<number>-<kebab-case>.md`
- ADRs: `ADR-<number>-<kebab-case>.md`
- playbooks: `PB-<number>-<kebab-case>.md`
- schemas: `<entity>.schema.json`
- rules: `<domain>.rules.yaml`
- stable IDs: uppercase family prefix plus non-reused identifier

Names SHALL use canonical glossary terms, avoid provider names outside adapters, and remain independent of mutable labels, selectors, paths, or storage keys.
