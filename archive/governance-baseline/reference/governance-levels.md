# Governance Levels

| Level | Meaning | Minimum Control |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | Observation | Provenance and Workspace scope |
| G1 | Candidate | Owner, evidence, uncertainty, expiry |
| G2 | Reviewed draft | Required reviewers and resolved blocking findings |
| G3 | Accepted authority | Approval and quality-gate evidence |
| G4 | Operational authority | Accepted artifact plus verified runtime evidence |

Lower levels SHALL NOT satisfy dependencies requiring a higher level.
